# CareSync

**Your intelligent, all-in-one companion for medication safety, health monitoring, and emergency preparedness.**

CareSync brings together medication management, biometric tracking, AI-powered clinical support, and emergency response tools into a single connected platform — helping patients, caregivers, and care teams stay in sync.

---

## 🚀 Features

### 💊 Medication Schedule & Adherence
Smart scheduling and reminders that track when medications are taken, missed, or due — helping patients stay consistent with their prescribed regimen.

### ❤️ Biometric Health Tracking
Log and visualize key vitals (heart rate, blood pressure, glucose, weight, etc.) over time to spot trends and flag anomalies early.

### 🆘 Emergency Medical ID
A quick-access digital medical profile containing allergies, conditions, medications, and emergency contacts — available instantly when it matters most.

### 👨‍⚕️ Care Team & Appointment Scheduler
Coordinate appointments, share updates with doctors and caregivers, and keep the entire care team aligned on one calendar.

### 🤖 AI Clinical Assistant & Doctor Visit Briefing
An AI assistant that summarizes health history, medication adherence, and symptoms into a concise briefing to prepare patients (and doctors) for visits.

### 📷 AI Prescription & Bottle Vision OCR
Scan prescription labels or pill bottles using computer vision + OCR to automatically extract dosage, frequency, and drug information — no manual entry needed.

### 🧩 Interactive 4-Slot Digital Pillbox
A virtual, interactive pillbox interface with four time-based slots (e.g., morning, afternoon, evening, night) to visually organize and confirm doses.

### ⚠️ Safety & Drug Matrix
An interaction-checking engine that cross-references medications to flag dangerous combinations, contraindications, and dosage conflicts.

### 🔊 Voice Audio Regimen Assistant
Voice-guided reminders and audio walkthroughs of the medication regimen — built for accessibility and hands-free use.

### 📝 Symptom & Adverse Reaction Diary
A structured log for tracking symptoms and side effects over time, helping identify patterns linked to specific medications or dosages.

### 📊 Cardiovascular & Metabolic Wellness Index
A composite health score derived from biometric data to give users an at-a-glance view of their cardiovascular and metabolic wellness.

### 📱 First Responder Emergency QR Beacon
A scannable QR code that gives first responders instant access to critical medical information during emergencies — even if the patient is unresponsive.
